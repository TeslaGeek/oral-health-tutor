Privacy policy

Oral Health Tutor is hosted on a secure web platform in the London region.

We do not intentionally collect direct personal identifiers such as names, email addresses, or passwords through this prototype website.

However, our hosting platform processes limited technical log data when you use the site, including IP address, date and time of access, requested URLs, referrer information, and browser/device information.

We use this information only to operate, secure, monitor, and troubleshoot the service.

Our lawful basis for this processing is legitimate interests.

Access to logs is restricted to authorised personnel and retained only for as long as necessary for operational and security purposes.

The online service provider host acts as a processor for personal data under its Data Processing Agreement.

If you have questions about privacy, contact: info [at] iesltd [dot] uk.
